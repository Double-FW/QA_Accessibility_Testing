# Agent workflow for connected reporting

This is a reusable prompt and workflow for an AI assistant or reporting system. It does not execute tests, connect a project tool, recruit customers or schedule monthly reports by itself.

## Required inputs

Obtain service facts, jurisdictions, decision purpose, relevant obligation decisions, product/build and journeys, declared baselines, existing test/evidence records, project workflow, authorised actions, aggregate customer demographics/context information and any approved research data. Read the root AGENTS.md, README authority model and relevant delivery framework sections.

Work with what is evidenced. If critical applicability, source, permission or research safeguards are missing, record the gap and the responsible review action. Continue independent drafting without inventing missing data.

## Copyable reporting prompt

> Prepare a connected accessibility report for the specified service.
>
> 1. Establish actual service, audience, channels, countries, builds, scope, dates and decision. Retrieve current Double-FW regulatory guidance and authoritative sources for each potentially applicable framework. Draft an obligation register, separating confirmed, excluded-with-reason and unresolved applicability; identify the authorised reviewer.
> 2. Select canonical user outcomes and exact source references, then retrieve the WCAG relationship register and relevant procedures. Preserve source revisions and namespaces. Create local supplementary tests for uncovered obligations. Produce a requirement-to-test coverage matrix and reproducible service-specific scripts. Do not claim execution from a plan.
> 3. Ingest actual execution evidence using the governed result statuses. Retain unknowns, exclusions, environment and sampling limits. Separate observed findings, inferred impact and proposed remedies.
> 4. Apply the organisation's severity/priority rubric, or clearly propose the example rubric for adoption. Apply urgent harm, essential-access and deadline overrides first. Record reach, frequency, context, uncertainty and root-cause dependencies.
> 5. Strongly recommend actionable records in the organisation's project management system. If authorised/configured, deduplicate and create/update tickets in the specified project, retain returned IDs and verify writes. Otherwise provide draft/import records and state no tickets were written. Never include participant identities or diagnoses in tickets.
> 6. Produce functional persona views for relevant Annex I VII lenses and intersections. Keep privacy cross-cutting. Include possible condition associations without diagnosing customers. Estimate marginal reach only with matched evidence and denominators; use unknowns/bounds for gaps and overlap. Never generate synthetic satisfaction as real research.
> 7. Compare actual completion, effort, privacy and aspect-specific satisfaction across comparable tasks/builds/profiles. Report sample, distributions, uncertainty and missing perspectives. Recommend safeguarded quantitative research where evidence is missing, and an appropriate A/B plan before material changes.
> 8. Join product/context evidence to findings. Estimate situational exposure, customer benefit, reach and commercial/engagement opportunity with clear assumptions. Avoid independence assumptions and double counting.
> 9. Project age-related needs using current official projections, compatible age-specific functional rates and aggregate customer composition where available. Separate market, customer-cohort and business scenarios; show sensitivity and source limitations.
> 10. Reconcile the monthly backlog snapshot, report verified fixes and inflow separately, show owners/dates and a conditional clearance forecast. Identify urgent actions and statutory outputs on their own timetables.
> 11. Produce evidence-linked reports and an action list. Distinguish technical conformance, broader outcomes, equality/adjustment assessment and satisfaction. Identify human review and decision rights; do not approve risk or claim legal compliance without the required authority and evidence.

## Review before delivery

Check internal references; source identities/revisions; actual versus proposed results; formula units and denominators; group overlap; privacy/disclosure; missing-data handling; backlog reconciliation; ticket write outcomes; statutory format/date; and bounded claims. The accountable reviewer must assess legal interpretation, significant evidence gaps, release decisions and exceptions.

Outputs may be Markdown or another accessible organisational format. These templates define recommended information, not an enforced data schema. Store sensitive evidence in approved controlled systems and link only safe references.

For ongoing operation, configure the organisation's own scheduler and approved connectors separately, with monthly cut-off, owner, failure notification, access controls and audit trail. This repository change does not activate an automation.
