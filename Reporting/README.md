# Regulatory reporting and remediation

Version 1.0 · 16 November 2026 · Practitioner guidance

Use this folder to turn an actual service scope and applicable obligations into a tailored test plan, evidence pack and managed remediation backlog. It supports human teams and AI-assisted workflows. It contains guidance and templates, not an installed reporting service, ticket integration or test runner.

## Start here

1. Follow [service scope and test scripts](Service_Scope_and_Test_Scripts.md) to establish applicable frameworks and coverage.
2. Use [prioritisation, tickets and monthly reporting](Prioritisation_Tickets_and_Monthly_Reporting.md) to make findings actionable.
3. Use [the agent workflow](Agent_Workflow.md) to generate drafts while preserving evidence and authority.
4. Add [persona-led experience](../Persona_Led_Reporting/README.md), [situational impact](../Situation_and_Environmental_Impact/README.md) and [age projections](../Age_Related_Impact_Projection/README.md) to explain who benefits, where and over what period.
5. Consult [sources and legal boundaries](Sources_and_Legal_Boundaries.md) before any regulatory claim.

All four reporting views share service, journey, requirement, test, execution and issue identifiers. A legal obligation, a technical result, a customer experience finding and a commercial opportunity remain separate records. Link them; do not convert them into a single compliance percentage.

## Governance

The [delivery framework](../Accessibility_Test_Led_Delivery_Framework.md), [testing approach](../Testing_Approach.md) and [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) continue to govern evidence and decisions. Existing canonical tests and the WCAG relationship register retain their authority. These reporting methods are derived practitioner guidance; score weights and templates are proposed local practice, not statutory thresholds.

A monthly internal report is strongly recommended. It does not replace a statutory submission, public accessibility statement, service conformity information, procurement ACR or immediate notification/corrective-action duty. Each has its own scope, recipient, required content and timetable.

## Common report header

Every report should identify report ID and revision; owner and intended decision; reporting dates and time zone; service/build and channels; geographies and customer denominator; included/excluded journeys; evidence cut-off; framework versions; source revisions; methods; known gaps; reviewer; and the date/trigger for review.

Keep observations, participant reports, estimates, hypotheses and recommendations labelled. Use “unknown” or “not evaluated” where evidence is absent. Publish accessible reports with meaningful headings, readable tables, text equivalents for charts and no colour-only status.
