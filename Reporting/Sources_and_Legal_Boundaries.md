# Sources, reporting routes and legal boundaries

Reviewed 16 November 2026. These are source starting points and practitioner summaries, not an applicability decision for a particular organisation. Recheck law, national implementation, commencement, standards citations and reporting requirements before use.

## Distinct legal and reporting routes

| Framework | What to verify and report | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [EAA: Directive 2019/882](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/882/oj) | Article 2 scope; national law; applicable Annex I requirements; service information under Article 13/Annex V; product-specific conformity routes where applicable; corrective action and competent authority requirements | Not a general law for every website. Annex I VII criteria have conditions of use. A survey or WCAG report does not alone establish conformity |
| [WAD: Directive 2016/2102](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2016/2102/oj) | National public-sector scope; applicable EN provisions and citation; accessibility statement, feedback and enforcement route | Member State monitoring reports and each body's public statement are different deliverables |
| [UK public-sector accessibility regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/952/contents) | Actual scope, applicable assessment guidance, accessibility statement, feedback and review | Separate duties may also arise under equality law |
| [Equality Act 2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/15/contents) | Relevant discrimination, service and reasonable-adjustment duties, including sections 20 and 29 where applicable; evidence of barriers, disadvantage, adjustments and effective access | Correct name is Equality Act 2010. It applies in Great Britain; Northern Ireland has a different framework. No universal satisfaction pass score or general monthly filing duty |
| [Revised Section 508 standards](https://www.access-board.gov/ict/) | Federal ICT scope, technical provisions, E204 functional criteria, equivalent facilitation where relevant, software/support and procurement requirements | Not a universal private-sector website law. Apply exact incorporated versions and scoping. An ACR supports due diligence; it is not certification or a survey substitute |
| Other global frameworks | Retrieve each applicable entry and official sources from the global overview; record local statements, plans, progress returns, regulator formats and deadlines | Do not reuse another jurisdiction's form or threshold without checking |

For WAD statements consult [Decision 2018/1523](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2018/1523/oj); monitoring/reporting uses [Decision 2018/1524](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2018/1524/oj). For procurement reports consult [ITI's VPAT resource](https://www.itic.org/policy/accessibility/vpat), select the appropriate current template and report product version, methods, clause-level support and explanations. A completed VPAT is generally an Accessibility Conformance Report (ACR), not a legal certificate.

Section 508 includes functional access criteria where specified technical chapters do not address a function, alongside technical requirements and other provisions. Broader task usability evidence is useful, but “high satisfaction means Section 508 compliant” is unsupported. Likewise, an equality assessment considers actual disadvantage and effective adjustments, not only a WCAG checklist.

## Research and privacy sources

Use [EU GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) where applicable and current [ICO special-category guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/a-guide-to-lawful-basis/special-category-data/). The ICO guidance identifies the need for an Article 6 basis and separate Article 9 condition for special-category processing, with additional safeguards where required. Its guidance was updated in October 2026; verify the current UK position rather than assuming EU and UK rules remain identical.

The [rolling research method](../Persona_Led_Reporting/Rolling_Research_and_AB_Testing.md) supplies a protocol, not a declaration of GDPR compliance. A privacy owner must assess actual collection, providers and risks.

## Condition associations and demographic evidence

The functional profile table is a research design aid, not a clinical taxonomy. Supporting health-information starting points include [NHS vision loss](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/vision-loss/), [NHS hearing loss](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/hearing-loss/), [NHS arthritis](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/arthritis/), [NHS dysarthria](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dysarthria/), [NHS stammering](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/stammering/) and [Epilepsy Action photosensitivity](https://www.epilepsy.org.uk/info/seizure-triggers/photosensitive-epilepsy). These describe conditions, not population percentages for the EAA profiles.

Use functional diversity evidence and participant input before extending associations to a specific population. Record primary statistical sources and definitions for every numerical estimate. No profile prevalence, measured customer result or legal compliance outcome is asserted by this package.
